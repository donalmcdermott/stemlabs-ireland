A nonprofit for young builders in Ireland

We back young people in Ireland who build things.

STEM Labs Ireland gives technically-minded young people the resources, the peers, and the credibility to build real things, early.

01 About

A small organisation with a simple job.

STEM Labs Ireland exists for one reason: to find young people in Ireland who are building things, and take them seriously.

We run STEM Racing Ireland, host Breakpoint, and give out small grants with fast decisions and no bureaucracy.

02 Programs
2.1

STEM Racing Ireland

The Irish arm of the global STEM competition. Students aged 9-19 form teams to design, build, brand, and race miniature cars, the way a real F1 team runs a project.

2.2

Breakpoint

A recurring meetup in Galway for builders and doers. Technical founders, engineers, and scientists. An informal evening of good conversation and short demos.
